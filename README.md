# 2D World

Minecraft in 2D. You walk along one line and the camera sits behind you, so
you can see yourself. The world is the normal world, flattened into a slice.

## The data pack (`2dworld.zip`)

- Keeps you and every mob on one line.
- Clears the blocks between you and the camera.
- Looks north all the time, and puts the camera 8 blocks behind you.
- **A** and **D** walk you left and right.
- Falling does not hurt.

Add it when you make a world: **Data Packs**, then drag the zip into the
window. It also works on a multiplayer server. When you join, press **F5**
until you can see yourself.

## The mod (optional)

The mod turns the camera fully sideways so you see yourself from the side, and
lets you turn to face the way you walk. It switches on by itself whenever the
data pack is in the world. The data pack works fine without it.
