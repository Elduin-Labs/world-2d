# 2D World

Minecraft in 2D. You walk along one line, and the camera sits to the side so
you can see yourself. The world is the normal world, flattened into a slice.

It comes in two parts:

- **The data pack** (`world-2d-data-pack.zip`) keeps you and every mob on one
  line, and clears the blocks between you and the camera.
- **The mod** turns the camera sideways and makes **A** walk left and **D**
  walk right. **W** or **Space** jumps.

Add the data pack when you make a world. The mod switches itself on whenever
that data pack is in the world.

Made by Elduin. Fabric, for Minecraft 26.3.
